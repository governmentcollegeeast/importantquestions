Discuss the Types and Determinants of Investment. Suggest Measures to Raise It and Explain Its Importance for the Country
Government College Ludhiana East • Macro Economics — B.Com (Sem II) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Investment is a crucial component of aggregate demand and plays a vital role in economic growth and development. It refers to expenditure on capital goods such as machinery, buildings, and infrastructure, which help in increasing future production capacity of the economy.

Types of Investment

  • Fixed Investment: Expenditure on fixed assets like machinery, plant, and buildings.
  • Inventory Investment: Investment in stock of goods held by firms.
  • Autonomous Investment: Investment independent of income level.
  • Induced Investment: Investment influenced by changes in income.
  • Public Investment: Investment made by government.
  • Private Investment: Investment made by private sector.

Determinants of Investment

  • Rate of Interest: Lower interest rates encourage investment.
  • Marginal Efficiency of Capital (MEC): Higher expected returns increase investment.
  • Business Expectations: Optimism about future demand promotes investment.
  • Level of Income: Higher income leads to higher demand and investment.
  • Government Policies: Taxation and incentives affect investment decisions.
  • Technological Changes: Innovations create new investment opportunities.

Explanation

Investment depends mainly on the relationship between the rate of interest and the marginal efficiency of capital. When expected returns are higher than the interest rate, investment takes place. If the interest rate is high, investment decreases, and vice versa.

Investment is also influenced by expectations and economic conditions. During periods of economic stability and growth, investment increases, while during uncertainty, it declines.

Measures to Increase Investment

  • Reduction in interest rates
  • Provision of tax incentives
  • Improvement in infrastructure
  • Stable economic policies
  • Encouragement of innovation
  • Easy availability of credit

Importance of Investment

  • Promotes economic growth
  • Generates employment opportunities
  • Increases production capacity
  • Leads to technological development
  • Improves standard of living

Conclusion

Investment is a key driver of economic development. It depends on various economic factors and plays a crucial role in increasing income, employment, and overall growth of the country. Proper policies and favorable conditions can significantly enhance investment levels.

Explain the Techniques of Executive Development
Government College Ludhiana East • Human Resource Management — B.Com (Sem II) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Executive development is a systematic process of enhancing the skills, knowledge, and abilities of managerial personnel. In the modern business environment, organizations require competent executives who can make effective decisions and lead the organization towards success. Therefore, various techniques of executive development are used to improve managerial efficiency and leadership qualities.

Meaning of Executive Development

Executive development refers to the process of improving managerial skills and competencies through various training and development programmes. It focuses on preparing executives for higher responsibilities and future challenges.

Techniques of Executive Development

The techniques of executive development can be broadly classified into on-the-job and off-the-job methods:

1. On-the-Job Techniques

  • Job Rotation:
    Executives are moved from one department to another to gain diverse experience and understanding of the organization.
  • Coaching:
    Senior managers guide and train executives by providing advice, feedback, and support.
  • Understudy Method:
    A subordinate is trained to take over the position of a superior in the future.
  • Committee Assignments:
    Executives are assigned to committees to participate in decision-making and problem-solving.
  • Project Assignment:
    Executives are given specific projects to handle independently, enhancing their decision-making skills.

2. Off-the-Job Techniques

  • Lectures and Seminars:
    Formal sessions to provide theoretical knowledge and insights.
  • Case Study Method:
    Analysis of real-life business situations to develop problem-solving skills.
  • Role Playing:
    Executives act out roles in simulated situations to improve interpersonal skills.
  • Business Games:
    Simulated business scenarios to enhance decision-making and strategic thinking.
  • Sensitivity Training:
    Focuses on improving interpersonal relations and understanding behavior.
  • Management Development Programmes (MDPs):
    Structured programmes conducted by institutions to develop managerial skills.

Importance of Executive Development

  • Develops leadership and managerial skills
  • Prepares executives for higher positions
  • Improves decision-making ability
  • Enhances organizational efficiency
  • Ensures continuity of management

Limitations of Executive Development

  • Costly and time-consuming
  • Results are not immediately visible
  • Possibility of ineffective training methods
  • Resistance from executives

Explanation

Executive development is essential for the growth and success of an organization. It helps in developing competent leaders who can handle complex situations and make strategic decisions. Both on-the-job and off-the-job techniques play a significant role in enhancing managerial capabilities.

Organizations must carefully select appropriate techniques based on their needs and objectives to ensure effective executive development.

Conclusion

In conclusion, executive development is a vital function of HRM that focuses on improving managerial skills and preparing executives for future challenges. By adopting suitable techniques, organizations can build a strong leadership team and achieve long-term success.

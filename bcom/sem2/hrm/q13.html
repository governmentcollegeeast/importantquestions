What do you mean by Performance Appraisal? Explain the Techniques of Performance Appraisal
Government College Ludhiana East • Human Resource Management — B.Com (Sem II) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Performance appraisal is a vital function of Human Resource Management that involves evaluating the performance of employees in a systematic manner. It helps organizations to assess employee efficiency, identify strengths and weaknesses, and make important decisions regarding promotion, training, and compensation.

Meaning of Performance Appraisal

Performance appraisal refers to the systematic evaluation of an employee’s performance in terms of job requirements, skills, and overall contribution to the organization.

It is a continuous process that aims at improving employee performance and achieving organizational goals.

Objectives of Performance Appraisal

  • To evaluate employee performance
  • To identify training and development needs
  • To provide feedback to employees
  • To determine promotions and salary increments
  • To improve organizational efficiency

Techniques of Performance Appraisal

Various techniques are used to evaluate employee performance effectively:

1. Traditional Methods

  • Ranking Method: Employees are ranked from best to worst based on performance.
  • Grading Method: Employees are graded as excellent, good, average, or poor.
  • Graphic Rating Scale: Performance is evaluated on a scale for different traits.
  • Checklist Method: A list of statements is used to assess employee performance.
  • Critical Incident Method: Focuses on important events that reflect employee behavior.
  • Essay Method: The appraiser writes a detailed report on employee performance.

2. Modern Methods

  • Management by Objectives (MBO): Performance is evaluated based on achievement of set objectives.
  • 360-Degree Feedback: Feedback is collected from superiors, peers, subordinates, and customers.
  • Assessment Centre Method: Employees are evaluated through simulations and exercises.
  • Human Resource Accounting Method: Evaluates employee contribution in monetary terms.
  • Behaviourally Anchored Rating Scale (BARS): Combines rating scales with specific behavioral examples.

Advantages of Performance Appraisal

  • Improves employee performance
  • Provides basis for promotion and rewards
  • Identifies training needs
  • Enhances communication between management and employees
  • Increases motivation and job satisfaction

Limitations of Performance Appraisal

  • Possibility of bias and subjectivity
  • Time-consuming process
  • May create dissatisfaction among employees
  • Difficulty in measuring performance accurately

Explanation

Performance appraisal is essential for evaluating employee performance and improving organizational efficiency. Different techniques provide various approaches to assessment, ranging from simple ranking methods to advanced 360-degree feedback systems.

The effectiveness of performance appraisal depends on fairness, accuracy, and proper implementation of appraisal techniques.

Conclusion

In conclusion, performance appraisal is a crucial tool for managing human resources effectively. It helps organizations to evaluate, motivate, and develop employees. By using appropriate appraisal techniques, organizations can enhance employee performance and achieve long-term success.

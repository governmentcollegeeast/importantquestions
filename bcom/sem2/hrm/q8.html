Explain the Steps in Training Programme and Methods to Evaluate Training
Government College Ludhiana East • Human Resource Management — B.Com (Sem II) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Training is an essential function of Human Resource Management which aims at improving the knowledge, skills, and abilities of employees. A well-designed training programme ensures that employees perform their jobs efficiently and contribute to organizational success. However, for training to be effective, it must follow a systematic process and its outcomes must be properly evaluated.

Steps in Training Programme

The training programme is carried out through the following systematic steps:

  • 1. Identification of Training Needs:
    The first step is to determine the areas where training is required. This can be done through performance appraisal, observation, and feedback.
  • 2. Setting Training Objectives:
    Clear and specific objectives are defined to guide the training programme and measure its effectiveness.
  • 3. Designing Training Programme:
    This involves deciding the content, methods, duration, and resources required for training.
  • 4. Selection of Trainees:
    Employees who require training are selected based on job requirements and performance gaps.
  • 5. Selection of Training Methods:
    Appropriate training methods such as lectures, demonstrations, or on-the-job training are chosen.
  • 6. Implementation of Training:
    The training programme is conducted as per the plan, ensuring active participation of trainees.
  • 7. Evaluation of Training:
    The effectiveness of the training programme is assessed to determine whether the objectives have been achieved.

Methods to Evaluate Training

Evaluation of training is necessary to measure its effectiveness and identify areas for improvement. The following methods are commonly used:

  • 1. Reaction Method:
    Measures the trainees’ satisfaction and feedback regarding the training programme.
  • 2. Learning Method:
    Assesses the knowledge and skills gained by trainees through tests and assessments.
  • 3. Behaviour Method:
    Evaluates changes in employee behavior and performance after training.
  • 4. Results Method:
    Measures the overall impact of training on organizational performance such as productivity and profitability.
  • 5. Cost-Benefit Analysis:
    Compares the cost of training with the benefits derived from it.

Importance of Training and Evaluation

  • Improves employee performance and productivity
  • Enhances skills and knowledge
  • Reduces errors and accidents
  • Increases employee morale and satisfaction
  • Ensures effective utilization of training resources

Limitations of Training Programme

  • Costly and time-consuming
  • Difficulty in measuring results accurately
  • Resistance from employees
  • Possibility of ineffective training methods

Explanation

A training programme is effective only when it is properly planned, implemented, and evaluated. Each step plays a crucial role in ensuring that employees acquire the required skills and knowledge. Evaluation helps in identifying the strengths and weaknesses of the programme and provides feedback for improvement.

Thus, training and its evaluation together ensure continuous development of employees and contribute to organizational growth.

Conclusion

In conclusion, training programmes are essential for enhancing employee capabilities and achieving organizational goals. A systematic approach to training, along with proper evaluation methods, ensures that the desired outcomes are achieved. Despite certain limitations, training remains a vital tool for organizational development and success.

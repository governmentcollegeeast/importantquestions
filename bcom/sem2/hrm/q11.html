Explain the Different Methods of Transfer
Government College Ludhiana East • Human Resource Management — B.Com (Sem II) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Transfer is an important function of Human Resource Management which involves shifting an employee from one job, department, or location to another without any significant change in rank or salary. It is used to balance workforce, improve efficiency, and meet organizational requirements. Various methods of transfer are adopted by organizations depending on their objectives and situations.

Meaning of Transfer

Transfer refers to the movement of an employee from one position to another within the organization where the pay, status, and responsibilities remain almost the same.

It helps in maintaining flexibility and ensuring proper utilization of human resources.

Methods / Types of Transfer

  • 1. Production Transfer:
    Employees are transferred from one department to another to meet production requirements or balance workload.
  • 2. Replacement Transfer:
    An employee is transferred to replace another employee who is leaving, promoted, or absent.
  • 3. Remedial Transfer:
    Transfer made to correct wrong placement or improve employee performance by placing them in a suitable job.
  • 4. Versatility Transfer:
    Employees are transferred to different jobs to develop multiple skills and increase flexibility.
  • 5. Shift Transfer:
    Employees are shifted from one shift to another (e.g., day shift to night shift) based on organizational needs.
  • 6. Penalty Transfer:
    Transfer made as a disciplinary action for misconduct or poor performance.

Purpose of Transfer

  • To meet organizational requirements
  • To balance workforce among departments
  • To improve employee skills and versatility
  • To correct wrong placements
  • To maintain discipline and control

Advantages of Transfer

  • Improves employee efficiency
  • Reduces monotony and boredom
  • Develops multi-skilled workforce
  • Helps in better utilization of human resources
  • Promotes flexibility in the organization

Limitations of Transfer

  • May cause dissatisfaction among employees
  • Adjustment problems in new job or location
  • Temporary decrease in productivity
  • Possibility of misuse by management

Explanation

Transfer is a useful tool for managing human resources effectively. It helps organizations respond to changing needs and ensures that employees are placed where they can perform best. However, improper transfers may lead to dissatisfaction and reduced morale.

Therefore, transfers should be planned carefully, considering both organizational objectives and employee interests.

Conclusion

In conclusion, transfer is an important HRM practice that facilitates efficient utilization of employees and organizational flexibility. Different methods of transfer serve various purposes such as improving performance, balancing workload, and maintaining discipline. When implemented properly, transfer contributes to both employee development and organizational success.

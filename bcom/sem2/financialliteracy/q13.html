“Commercial Bank is the Foundation Stone of Modern Commerce and Industry.” Explain the Statement.
Government College Ludhiana East • Financial Literacy — B.Com Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Commercial banks occupy a central position in the modern economic system. In the present age of industrialization and globalization, trade and industrial activities cannot function efficiently without proper banking facilities. Commercial banks provide financial assistance, credit facilities, payment services, and various modern banking services that support business and industrial growth.

Due to their significant contribution in financing trade, industry, and economic development, commercial banks are rightly regarded as the “foundation stone of modern commerce and industry.” They mobilize savings from the public and channelize these funds into productive business activities.

Meaning of Commercial Bank

A commercial bank is a financial institution that accepts deposits from the public and provides loans and other banking services for the purpose of earning profit.

Commercial banks act as intermediaries between savers and borrowers and facilitate smooth financial transactions in the economy.

Why Commercial Banks are Called the Foundation Stone of Modern Commerce and Industry

Commercial banks play a vital role in the growth and development of trade and industrial activities. Their importance can be explained through the following points:

1. Provision of Credit Facilities

Commercial banks provide loans and advances to traders, businessmen, and industrialists for carrying out business operations and expansion activities.

Industries require huge amounts of capital for purchasing machinery, raw materials, and meeting working capital requirements. Commercial banks fulfill these financial needs through various credit facilities.

Without bank credit, large-scale industrial and commercial activities would not be possible.

2. Mobilization of Savings

Commercial banks encourage people to deposit their savings in banks by providing safety and interest on deposits.

These savings are then utilized for productive investments in commerce and industry.

Thus, banks help in converting idle savings into active capital for economic development.

3. Promotion of Trade and Commerce

Commercial banks facilitate domestic and international trade by providing services such as letters of credit, bill discounting, bank guarantees, and foreign exchange services.

These services help businessmen conduct trade activities smoothly and securely.

4. Facilitation of Payment System

Commercial banks provide modern payment facilities such as cheques, drafts, internet banking, mobile banking, and electronic fund transfers.

These services ensure quick and safe transfer of money, which is essential for commercial transactions.

5. Promotion of Capital Formation

Banks collect scattered savings from the public and provide funds for productive investment purposes.

This process increases capital formation, which is necessary for industrial growth and economic development.

6. Support to Industrial Development

Commercial banks provide both short-term and medium-term finance to industries.

They help industries purchase machinery, adopt modern technology, and expand production activities.

This contributes significantly to industrial development and employment generation.

7. Assistance to Agriculture and Small Businesses

Commercial banks provide loans to farmers, small entrepreneurs, and small-scale industries.

This promotes rural development, agricultural productivity, and growth of small businesses.

8. Contribution to Economic Development

Commercial banks contribute to economic growth by promoting investment, employment, production, and trade activities.

They play an important role in maintaining financial stability and supporting overall economic progress.

9. Promotion of International Trade

Commercial banks provide foreign exchange facilities and financial support for import and export activities.

They help businesses engage in international trade and global business operations.

10. Generation of Employment Opportunities

By financing industries and business activities, commercial banks indirectly create employment opportunities in different sectors of the economy.

Expansion of industries and trade leads to increased job opportunities and higher income levels.

Conclusion

Commercial banks are truly regarded as the foundation stone of modern commerce and industry because they provide essential financial services that support trade, industrial growth, and economic development. They mobilize savings, provide credit facilities, facilitate financial transactions, and encourage investment activities.

Without the support of commercial banks, modern business and industrial operations would face serious difficulties. Therefore, commercial banks play an indispensable role in the growth and prosperity of modern economies.

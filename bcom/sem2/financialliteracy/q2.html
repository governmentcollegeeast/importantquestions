Describe Savings. Explain the Various Avenues for Savings from the Surplus Funds.
Government College Ludhiana East • Financial Literacy — B.Com Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Savings play a vital role in financial stability and economic development. In modern financial life, individuals must develop the habit of saving a part of their income for future needs and emergencies. Savings provide financial security, reduce dependence on borrowing, and help individuals achieve long-term financial goals.

When income exceeds expenditure, the remaining amount is known as surplus funds. These surplus funds should not remain idle; instead, they should be invested or saved in suitable avenues that provide safety, liquidity, and returns. Therefore, understanding savings and different avenues for savings is an essential aspect of financial literacy.

Meaning of Savings

Savings refer to that portion of income which is not spent on current consumption and is set aside for future use. It is the excess of income over expenditure.

In simple words, savings mean postponing present consumption in order to secure future financial benefits.

Savings help individuals meet emergencies, fulfill future goals, and maintain financial independence.

Definition of Savings

According to economics, savings are defined as:

“The portion of disposable income that is not spent on consumption.”

Thus, savings represent the amount retained after meeting day-to-day expenses.

Importance of Savings

  • Financial Security: Savings provide protection during emergencies such as illness, unemployment, or accidents.
  • Achievement of Future Goals: Savings help in fulfilling future objectives such as education, marriage, house purchase, or retirement.
  • Reduction in Financial Stress: Individuals with savings are less dependent on loans and credit.
  • Capital Formation: Savings contribute to investment and economic growth.
  • Support During Inflation: Savings help individuals cope with rising prices.
  • Encourages Financial Discipline: Regular saving habits improve money management skills.

Features of Good Savings

  • Safety of principal amount
  • Reasonable rate of return
  • Liquidity or easy conversion into cash
  • Stability and reliability
  • Protection against risk
  • Convenience of investment

Various Avenues for Savings from Surplus Funds

There are different avenues available for investing or saving surplus funds. Individuals select these avenues according to their financial goals, risk-bearing capacity, and return expectations.

Important avenues for savings are explained below:

1. Savings Bank Account

A savings bank account is one of the safest and most common avenues for savings.

Individuals deposit their surplus money in banks and earn interest on the deposited amount.

It provides high liquidity because money can be withdrawn easily whenever required.

This avenue is suitable for short-term savings and emergency funds.

2. Fixed Deposit (FD)

A fixed deposit is a savings instrument in which money is deposited for a fixed period at a fixed rate of interest.

It provides higher returns compared to ordinary savings accounts.

Fixed deposits are considered safe because they are generally offered by banks and financial institutions.

However, liquidity is lower because premature withdrawal may attract penalties.

3. Recurring Deposit (RD)

A recurring deposit allows individuals to deposit a fixed amount regularly every month for a specified period.

It encourages disciplined savings habits among individuals.

At maturity, the depositor receives the deposited amount along with interest.

4. Post Office Savings Schemes

Post offices provide various savings schemes such as National Savings Certificate (NSC), Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP), and Post Office Monthly Income Scheme.

These schemes are backed by the government and are considered highly secure.

They are suitable for conservative investors who prefer safety over high returns.

5. Public Provident Fund (PPF)

Public Provident Fund is a long-term savings scheme introduced by the government.

It provides attractive interest rates along with tax benefits.

PPF is suitable for retirement planning and long-term wealth creation.

6. Mutual Funds

Mutual funds collect money from many investors and invest it in shares, bonds, and other securities.

Professional fund managers manage these investments on behalf of investors.

Mutual funds provide diversification and may generate higher returns compared to traditional savings avenues.

However, returns are subject to market risks.

7. Investment in Shares

Individuals may invest surplus funds in shares of companies through the stock market.

Shareholders earn returns in the form of dividends and capital appreciation.

Although shares may provide high returns, they involve higher risk due to market fluctuations.

8. Insurance Policies

Insurance policies not only provide financial protection against risks but also encourage long-term savings.

Life insurance policies help individuals build savings while providing security to family members.

9. Investment in Gold

Gold is considered a traditional and popular avenue for savings in India.

People invest in physical gold, gold bonds, or gold exchange traded funds.

Gold acts as a hedge against inflation and economic uncertainty.

10. Real Estate

Investment in land and property is another avenue for utilizing surplus funds.

Real estate investments may provide rental income and capital appreciation over time.

However, such investments require large amounts of capital and have lower liquidity.

Factors to be Considered While Choosing Savings Avenues

  • Safety of investment
  • Expected rate of return
  • Liquidity needs
  • Investment period
  • Tax benefits
  • Risk-bearing capacity of the investor
  • Financial goals and objectives

Conclusion

Savings are essential for financial security, future planning, and economic stability. They help individuals meet emergencies and achieve long-term financial objectives. Merely saving money is not sufficient; surplus funds should be invested wisely through suitable savings avenues.

Different avenues such as bank deposits, PPF, mutual funds, shares, gold, and real estate provide varying levels of safety, liquidity, and returns. Therefore, individuals should carefully select savings avenues according to their financial goals, risk tolerance, and future needs.

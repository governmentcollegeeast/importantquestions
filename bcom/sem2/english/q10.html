What is the Purpose of Writing a Letter? What are the Basic Parts of a Letter?
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Letter writing is one of the oldest and most important forms of written communication. Despite the rise of digital communication, letters continue to play a significant role in formal and informal interactions. A well-written letter conveys information clearly, maintains relationships, and reflects the writer’s personality and professionalism.

Meaning of Letter Writing

Letter writing refers to the process of communicating information, ideas, or feelings in a written format addressed to a specific person or organization. It can be formal or informal depending on the purpose and context.

Letters are widely used in business, education, and personal communication, making them an essential skill for students and professionals.

Purpose of Writing a Letter

  • Communication of Information: Letters are used to convey messages, information, or instructions.
  • Maintaining Relationships: Personal letters help in maintaining social and emotional connections.
  • Formal Communication: Letters are used for official purposes such as applications, complaints, and business communication.
  • Record Keeping: Written letters serve as a permanent record of communication.
  • Expression of Feelings: Letters allow individuals to express thoughts, emotions, and opinions clearly.

Basic Parts of a Letter

  • Sender’s Address: The address of the person writing the letter, usually written at the top.
  • Date: The date on which the letter is written.
  • Receiver’s Address: The address of the person or organization to whom the letter is addressed.
  • Subject: A brief statement indicating the purpose of the letter (mainly in formal letters).
  • Salutation: A greeting used to address the recipient, such as “Dear Sir/Madam”.
  • Body of the Letter: The main content where the message is explained clearly. It is usually divided into paragraphs.
  • Complimentary Close: A polite ending such as “Yours sincerely” or “Yours faithfully”.
  • Signature: The name and signature of the sender.

Note on Format

The format of a letter may vary depending on whether it is formal or informal. Formal letters follow a structured format with all the essential parts, while informal letters are more flexible in style and structure. However, clarity and proper organization are important in both types.

Explanation

Letter writing is not only a medium of communication but also a reflection of one’s writing skills and professionalism. A well-structured letter ensures that the message is conveyed effectively and leaves a positive impression on the reader.

In academic examinations, students are expected to follow proper format, maintain clarity, and use appropriate language while writing letters. Therefore, understanding the purpose and parts of a letter is essential.

Conclusion

In conclusion, letter writing is an important skill that serves various purposes, including communication, expression, and record keeping. A letter consists of several essential parts that ensure its clarity and effectiveness. By mastering the structure and purpose of letter writing, students can communicate more effectively in both personal and professional contexts.

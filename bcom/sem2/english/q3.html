Read the Passage Carefully and Answer the Questions that Follow (Reading Comprehension)
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Reading comprehension questions are designed to test a student’s ability to understand, interpret, and analyze a given passage. Unlike theoretical questions, this type of question requires practical application of reading skills. The student must read the passage carefully and answer the questions based strictly on the information provided.

Correct Approach to Solve a Comprehension Passage

  • Read the Passage Thoroughly: The passage should be read carefully at least twice to understand its overall meaning and tone.
  • Identify the Main Idea: Determine the central theme or message conveyed in the passage.
  • Focus on Important Details: Pay attention to key facts, examples, and arguments presented by the author.
  • Understand Vocabulary in Context: Difficult words should be interpreted based on the context in which they are used.
  • Analyze the Questions Carefully: Each question should be read properly to understand what exactly is being asked.
  • Answer Based on the Passage Only: Answers should be derived strictly from the given passage and not from personal opinions or outside knowledge.
  • Use Clear and Concise Language: Answers should be written in simple, precise, and grammatically correct sentences.
  • Maintain Sequence: Answers should follow the order of the questions for better presentation.
  • Review Answers: After completing, answers should be rechecked for accuracy and clarity.

Sample Passage

Education plays a vital role in shaping an individual’s personality and future. It not only imparts knowledge but also develops critical thinking and decision-making abilities. In the modern world, education has become essential for personal and professional growth. It helps individuals to adapt to changing circumstances and contribute effectively to society. However, education should not be limited to theoretical knowledge; it must also include practical skills and ethical values.

Questions Based on the Passage

  • What is the main purpose of education according to the passage?
  • How does education help in personal development?
  • Why is practical knowledge important?
  • Give a suitable title to the passage.

Suggested Answers

  • Main Purpose of Education: The main purpose of education is to shape an individual’s personality and prepare them for future challenges.
  • Role in Personal Development: Education develops critical thinking and decision-making abilities, which are essential for personal growth.
  • Importance of Practical Knowledge: Practical knowledge is important because it helps individuals apply theoretical concepts in real-life situations.
  • Suitable Title: “Importance of Education in Modern Life”.

Explanation

This type of question evaluates not only reading ability but also comprehension, interpretation, and expression skills. Students must ensure that their answers are accurate, relevant, and based on the given passage. The inclusion of a sample passage and answers helps in understanding how to approach such questions effectively in examinations.

Regular practice of comprehension passages improves reading speed, vocabulary, and analytical skills, which are crucial for academic success.

Conclusion

In conclusion, comprehension passages are an important component of English examinations that test a student’s practical understanding of a text. By following a systematic approach and focusing on clarity and accuracy, students can perform well in such questions. Therefore, mastering comprehension skills is essential for achieving success in academic examinations.

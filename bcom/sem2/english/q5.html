Write a Short Note on Paraphrasing. What are the Important Guidelines for Paraphrasing?
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

In academic writing, it is often necessary to restate ideas in a different form without changing their original meaning. This skill is known as paraphrasing. It plays a crucial role in enhancing comprehension, avoiding plagiarism, and expressing ideas in a clear and effective manner. Paraphrasing requires both understanding and linguistic ability, making it an essential component of language learning.

Meaning of Paraphrasing

Paraphrasing is the process of rewriting a given text or passage in one’s own words while retaining the original meaning and idea. It involves changing the wording, structure, and style of the original text without altering its essence.

Unlike summarising, which shortens the text, paraphrasing maintains approximately the same length as the original passage but presents it in a different way. It requires a deep understanding of the text and the ability to express the same idea differently.

Thus, paraphrasing can be defined as the art of restating information in a new form while preserving its original meaning.

Purpose of Paraphrasing

  • To Improve Understanding: Paraphrasing helps in better comprehension of the original text.
  • To Avoid Plagiarism: It allows students to use ideas from a source without copying directly.
  • To Simplify Complex Ideas: Difficult concepts can be expressed in simpler and clearer language.
  • To Enhance Writing Skills: It improves vocabulary and sentence construction.
  • To Present Information Effectively: Paraphrasing helps in presenting ideas in a manner suitable for the intended audience.

Important Guidelines for Paraphrasing

  • Understand the Original Text Clearly: The passage should be read carefully to grasp its complete meaning before rewriting.
  • Use Your Own Words: The text should be rewritten in original language rather than copying phrases from the source.
  • Change Sentence Structure: The structure of sentences should be altered to create a new version of the text.
  • Maintain Original Meaning: The paraphrased version must convey the same idea as the original text.
  • Use Synonyms Appropriately: Suitable synonyms should be used without distorting the meaning.
  • Avoid Word-to-Word Substitution: Simply replacing words with synonyms is not effective paraphrasing.
  • Keep the Length Similar: The paraphrased text should be approximately equal in length to the original.
  • Ensure Clarity and Coherence: The rewritten text should be clear, logical, and grammatically correct.
  • Revise and Compare: The paraphrased version should be compared with the original to ensure accuracy.

Example of Paraphrasing

Original Sentence: Education is essential for personal growth and success in life.

Paraphrased Version: Education plays a vital role in the overall development of an individual and helps in achieving success.

Explanation

Paraphrasing is a skill that requires both comprehension and creativity. It enables students to internalize information and express it in their own unique style. This not only improves writing skills but also enhances confidence in communication.

In academic contexts, paraphrasing is widely used in assignments, reports, and examinations. It reflects a student’s ability to understand and reinterpret information effectively.

Conclusion

In conclusion, paraphrasing is an important language skill that involves rewriting information in a new form while retaining its original meaning. By following proper guidelines and practicing regularly, students can develop effective paraphrasing skills. This not only improves academic performance but also ensures ethical use of information and enhances overall communication abilities.

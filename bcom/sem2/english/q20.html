Applied Writing Practice — Paragraph, Notes, Resume and Report
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

In examinations, students are often required to apply their writing skills in practical situations. This includes paragraph writing, note-making, resume preparation, and report writing. This section provides model examples for each type to help students understand the correct approach.

Paragraph Writing (Example)

Topic: Importance of Time Management

Time management is an essential skill for achieving success in life. It helps individuals to plan their activities efficiently and complete tasks within deadlines. Proper management of time reduces stress and increases productivity. Students who manage their time effectively can balance studies and personal life easily. Therefore, time management plays a crucial role in achieving both academic and professional goals.

Note-Making (Example)

Topic: Benefits of Exercise

  • Improves physical health
  • Enhances mental well-being
  • Increases energy levels
  • Reduces risk of diseases
  • Promotes better lifestyle

Resume Writing (Example)

Post: Accountant

Name: Rahul Sharma
Email: rahul@gmail.com
Phone: 9876543210

Career Objective: To secure a position as an accountant where I can apply my financial and analytical skills.

Education:
B.Com, Panjab University (2024)

Skills:
Accounting, MS Excel, Financial Analysis

Experience:
Intern at XYZ Firm (3 months)

Report Writing (Example)

Title: Report on Cleanliness Drive

A cleanliness drive was organized in the college campus on 10th March 2026. Students actively participated in cleaning the surroundings and spreading awareness about hygiene. The event was successful in promoting cleanliness and responsibility among students. It is recommended that such initiatives should be conducted regularly.

Explanation

Each example demonstrates the correct format and approach required in examinations. Paragraph writing focuses on unity and clarity, note-making emphasizes brevity, resume writing highlights structured information, and report writing presents facts systematically.

Conclusion

Applied writing skills are essential for academic and professional success. By practicing different formats such as paragraphs, notes, resumes, and reports, students can improve their writing abilities and perform effectively in examinations.

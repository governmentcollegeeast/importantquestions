What is Summarising? Explain the Features of a Good Summary
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

In academic writing and communication, the ability to present information in a brief and clear manner is highly valued. Summarising is an essential skill that allows a reader to condense a lengthy passage into its most important points without losing its original meaning. It plays a significant role in enhancing comprehension, retention, and effective communication.

Meaning of Summarising

Summarising is the process of expressing the main ideas of a text in a concise and coherent form. It involves reducing the length of the original passage while retaining its essential information and overall message.

A summary is not a mere shortening of the text; rather, it is a thoughtful rewriting in which unnecessary details, examples, and repetitions are eliminated, and only the core ideas are presented in a clear and logical manner.

Thus, summarising can be defined as the art of presenting the essence of a text in a brief and precise form.

Features of a Good Summary

  • Conciseness: A good summary is brief and to the point. It eliminates unnecessary details and focuses only on the essential ideas.
  • Clarity: The language used in the summary should be clear and easy to understand. Ambiguity and confusion must be avoided.
  • Accuracy: The summary must faithfully represent the original text without distorting its meaning.
  • Coherence: The ideas should be presented in a logical and well-organized manner so that the summary flows smoothly.
  • Objectivity: A summary should be free from personal opinions, interpretations, or comments. It should reflect only the author’s ideas.
  • Use of Own Words: The summary should be written in the reader’s own words rather than copying sentences directly from the original text.
  • Completeness: All the important points of the original passage should be included, while minor details are omitted.
  • Proper Length: A summary is generally one-third of the original text, depending on the requirement.

Guidelines for Writing a Summary

  • Read the Passage Carefully: The text should be read thoroughly to understand its overall meaning.
  • Identify Main Ideas: Important points and key concepts should be underlined or noted.
  • Eliminate Unnecessary Details: Examples, illustrations, and repetitions should be removed.
  • Organize Ideas: The main ideas should be arranged logically.
  • Write in Own Words: The summary should be expressed in simple and original language.
  • Maintain Logical Flow: Sentences should be connected properly to ensure coherence.
  • Revise and Edit: The summary should be checked for grammatical accuracy and clarity.

Explanation

Summarising is not merely a mechanical process but a cognitive skill that requires understanding, analysis, and synthesis of information. It helps students to focus on key ideas and improves their ability to express thoughts clearly and effectively. In academic settings, summarising is often used for note-making, report writing, and examination purposes.

Moreover, the ability to summarise information accurately reflects a student’s comprehension level and analytical ability. Therefore, it is considered an important component of language learning.

Conclusion

In conclusion, summarising is a vital skill that enables individuals to condense information while preserving its essence. A good summary is concise, clear, accurate, and well-organized. By following proper guidelines and focusing on essential features, students can develop effective summarising skills, which are indispensable for academic success and professional communication.

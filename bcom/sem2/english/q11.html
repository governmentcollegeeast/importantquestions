Write about the Types of Letters
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Letter writing is an important form of communication that can be used for both personal and professional purposes. Depending on the nature, purpose, and relationship between the sender and the receiver, letters can be classified into different types. Understanding these types helps in selecting the appropriate style and tone for effective communication.

Classification of Letters

Letters are broadly classified into two main categories: Formal Letters and Informal Letters. Each type has its own format, tone, and purpose.

Formal Letters

Formal letters are written for official or professional purposes. They follow a fixed format and use formal language. These letters are addressed to people in authority or to organizations.

  • Business Letters: Written for business-related communication such as inquiries, orders, and complaints.
  • Official Letters: Used for communication with government offices or institutions.
  • Application Letters: Written when applying for a job, leave, or admission.
  • Complaint Letters: Written to express dissatisfaction regarding a product or service.
  • Inquiry Letters: Written to seek information.

Features of Formal Letters:

  • Use of formal and polite language
  • Proper format and structure
  • Clarity and precision
  • Objective tone (no personal emotions)

Informal Letters

Informal letters are written for personal communication with friends, family members, or relatives. They do not follow a strict format and allow freedom of expression.

  • Personal Letters: Written to share personal news, feelings, or experiences.
  • Friendly Letters: Used for casual communication with friends.
  • Invitation Letters: Written to invite someone to an event.

Features of Informal Letters:

  • Use of simple and friendly language
  • Flexible format
  • Personal tone and expression
  • Less emphasis on structure

Difference between Formal and Informal Letters

  • Purpose: Formal letters are used for official communication, while informal letters are used for personal communication.
  • Language: Formal letters use polite and professional language, whereas informal letters use casual language.
  • Format: Formal letters follow a fixed format, while informal letters have a flexible structure.
  • Tone: Formal letters are objective, while informal letters are personal and expressive.

Explanation

The choice of letter type depends on the situation and the relationship between the sender and the receiver. Using the correct type ensures that the message is conveyed appropriately and effectively. In academic examinations, students must identify the type of letter required and follow the correct format and tone.

Conclusion

In conclusion, letters can be broadly classified into formal and informal types, each serving a specific purpose. Understanding the differences between these types helps in effective communication. By choosing the appropriate format and language, one can ensure clarity and professionalism in letter writing.

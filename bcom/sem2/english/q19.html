Paraphrasing Practice — Rewrite the Given Passage
Government College Ludhiana East • English — B.Com (Sem I) Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Paraphrasing is an essential skill in academic writing that involves rewriting a passage in one’s own words while maintaining its original meaning. This section provides a model passage along with its paraphrased version to help students understand the correct method.

Original Passage

Education is the foundation of a successful life. It equips individuals with knowledge, skills, and values that help them face challenges and achieve their goals. In today’s competitive world, education is not just a necessity but a powerful tool for personal and professional growth.

Steps for Paraphrasing

1. Read the passage carefully: Understand the complete meaning.

2. Identify key ideas: Focus on the main message.

3. Use synonyms: Replace words with similar meanings.

4. Change sentence structure: Rewrite the sentence differently.

5. Maintain original meaning: Ensure the idea remains unchanged.

Paraphrased Version

Education forms the basis of a prosperous life. It provides individuals with the necessary knowledge, abilities, and values to overcome difficulties and accomplish their objectives. In the modern competitive era, education has become an essential and powerful means for both personal and professional development.

Explanation

The paraphrased version retains the original meaning but uses different words and sentence structure. Key terms such as “foundation” have been replaced with “basis,” and sentence patterns have been modified to create a new expression of the same idea. This demonstrates effective paraphrasing without copying the original text.

Conclusion

Paraphrasing requires both understanding and creativity. By practicing regularly and following proper steps, students can improve their ability to rewrite content effectively. This skill is essential for academic success and ethical writing.

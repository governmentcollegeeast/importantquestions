Introduction

In E-Commerce and online communication systems, security of information is extremely important. Businesses exchange sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, and customer data through digital networks. To protect this information from unauthorized access and cyber threats, cryptography is used.

Meaning of Cryptography

Cryptography refers to the process of converting readable information into coded or unreadable form to protect it from unauthorized users.

Definition: Cryptography is the science of securing information by converting data into coded form so that only authorized users can access and understand it.

The readable information is called Plain Text, while the coded information is known as Cipher Text.

Objectives of Cryptography

  • Protect confidentiality of information
  • Prevent unauthorized access
  • Ensure data integrity
  • Provide authentication of users
  • Secure online transactions

Types of Cryptography

  • 1. Private Key Cryptography: Uses a single secret key for encryption and decryption of information.
  • 2. Public Key Cryptography: Uses two keys — a public key for encryption and a private key for decryption.

Importance of Cryptography in E-Commerce

  • Protects online payment transactions
  • Secures customer information
  • Prevents hacking and cybercrime
  • Supports digital signatures
  • Ensures secure communication over networks

Advantages of Cryptography

  • Improves data security
  • Protects confidential information
  • Provides secure electronic communication
  • Enhances customer trust in E-Commerce
  • Reduces chances of online fraud

Limitations of Cryptography

  • Complex implementation process
  • Requires technical knowledge
  • High computational cost in some systems
  • Risk of key theft or misuse

Conclusion

Cryptography is an essential security technique used in E-Commerce and digital communication systems. It protects sensitive information by converting data into coded form and ensures confidentiality, integrity, and authentication in online transactions. As cyber threats continue to increase, cryptography plays a vital role in maintaining trust and security in the digital business environment.

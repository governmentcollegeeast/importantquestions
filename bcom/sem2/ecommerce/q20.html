Difference Between Pure Online and Brick & Click Business Model
Government College Ludhiana East • E-Commerce — BBA Semester II Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Modern businesses use different business models to conduct commercial activities in the digital marketplace. Some businesses operate completely online, while others combine physical stores with online platforms. Pure Online and Brick & Click are two important business models used in E-Commerce systems.

Meaning of Business Models

Pure Online Business Model: A business model where all operations and transactions are conducted only through online platforms without physical stores.

Brick & Click Business Model: A business model that combines physical stores (brick) with online business operations (click).

Difference Between Pure Online and Brick & Click Business Model

Basis Pure Online Business Model Brick & Click Business Model
Meaning Business operates completely through online platforms Business operates through both physical stores and online platforms
Physical Presence No physical stores available Physical stores are available
Customer Interaction Only online interaction with customers Both online and face-to-face interaction
Operational Cost Lower operational cost Higher operational cost due to physical stores
Market Reach Global market accessibility Both local and global market reach
Customer Trust May face trust issues initially Higher customer trust due to physical presence
Examples Online-only shopping websites Retail stores with online shopping facilities
Inventory Management Managed mainly through warehouses Managed through stores and warehouses
Business Operations Fully internet-based operations Combination of traditional and online operations

Advantages of Pure Online Business Model

  • Lower infrastructure cost
  • Global customer reach
  • 24×7 business operations
  • Easy scalability

Advantages of Brick & Click Business Model

  • Better customer trust and satisfaction
  • Multiple sales channels
  • Combination of online and offline services
  • Stronger brand presence

Conclusion

Pure Online and Brick & Click business models are important forms of E-Commerce business operations. Pure Online businesses operate completely through digital platforms, while Brick & Click businesses combine physical stores with online services. Both models have their own advantages and limitations. Businesses choose these models according to their objectives, customer needs, and market strategies.

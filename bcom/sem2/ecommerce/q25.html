Currency Servers
Government College Ludhiana East • E-Commerce — BBA Semester II Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

In international E-Commerce and online financial transactions, businesses and customers deal with multiple currencies from different countries. To manage currency exchange information and support global electronic transactions, currency servers are used. These systems provide updated exchange rates and currency conversion services for international online business activities.

Meaning of Currency Servers

Currency Servers are online systems or software applications that provide information regarding currency exchange rates and support currency conversion for international electronic transactions.

Definition: Currency Servers are digital systems that provide real-time exchange rate information and currency conversion services for electronic business transactions.

Functions of Currency Servers

  • Provide current foreign exchange rates
  • Support currency conversion
  • Assist international online transactions
  • Update exchange rate information regularly
  • Help businesses calculate international payments

Working of Currency Servers

User Enters Currency Information
↓
Currency Server Accesses Exchange Rates
↓
Currency Conversion Process
↓
Converted Currency Value Displayed

Importance of Currency Servers in E-Commerce

  • Support international E-Commerce transactions
  • Provide accurate exchange rate information
  • Help customers understand product pricing in local currency
  • Improve global business operations
  • Assist in online financial transactions

Advantages of Currency Servers

  • Fast currency conversion
  • Real-time exchange rate updates
  • Improved convenience in international trade
  • Better financial decision-making
  • Support for global business expansion

Limitations of Currency Servers

  • Dependence on internet connectivity
  • Exchange rates may fluctuate rapidly
  • Technical failures may affect transactions
  • Security risks in online financial systems

Conclusion

Currency Servers play an important role in international E-Commerce and online financial systems. They provide updated exchange rate information and support currency conversion for global business transactions. By improving the efficiency and accuracy of international payments, currency servers help businesses and customers participate effectively in the global digital economy.

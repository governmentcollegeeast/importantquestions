Shopping Cart
Government College Ludhiana East • E-Commerce — BBA Semester II Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

A shopping cart is one of the most important components of an E-Commerce website. It allows customers to select, store, and manage products before completing the online purchase process. Shopping cart systems make online shopping convenient, organized, and efficient for customers as well as businesses.

Meaning of Shopping Cart

A Shopping Cart is a software application used in E-Commerce websites that allows customers to collect and manage products they wish to purchase online.

Definition: A Shopping Cart is an online software tool that enables customers to select, review, modify, and purchase products through an E-Commerce website.

Features of Shopping Cart

  • Add products for purchase
  • Remove products from the cart
  • Update quantity of products
  • Display total cost of products
  • Provide secure checkout process
  • Support multiple payment options

Working of Shopping Cart

Customer Selects Product
↓
Product Added to Shopping Cart
↓
Customer Reviews Products
↓
Checkout & Payment Process
↓
Order Confirmation

Importance of Shopping Cart in E-Commerce

  • Provides convenience in online shopping
  • Helps customers manage selected products
  • Improves online purchasing experience
  • Supports secure online transactions
  • Increases efficiency of E-Commerce operations

Advantages of Shopping Cart

  • Easy product selection and management
  • Fast online purchasing process
  • Automatic calculation of total price
  • Supports multiple payment methods
  • Enhances customer satisfaction

Limitations of Shopping Cart

  • Technical errors may affect transactions
  • Security risks in online payments
  • Dependence on internet connectivity
  • Cart abandonment by customers

Conclusion

A Shopping Cart is an essential tool of E-Commerce that simplifies the online purchasing process for customers. It helps users select, manage, and purchase products conveniently through digital platforms. By improving customer experience and supporting secure online transactions, shopping carts play a vital role in the success of E-Commerce businesses.

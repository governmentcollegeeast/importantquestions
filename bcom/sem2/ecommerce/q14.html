E-Business Model
Government College Ludhiana East • E-Commerce — BBA Semester II Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

The development of internet technologies has transformed the traditional way of conducting business activities. Organizations now use online platforms and digital communication systems to interact with customers, suppliers, and business partners. This has led to the emergence of various E-Business models that help businesses conduct operations electronically and generate revenue efficiently.

Meaning of E-Business Model

An E-Business model refers to the method or framework through which a business conducts its activities electronically and earns revenue using internet technologies and digital platforms.

Definition: An E-Business model is a structure that explains how a business creates value, delivers products or services, and generates income through electronic systems and online platforms.

Types of E-Business Models

  • Business to Business (B2B): Transactions take place between one business organization and another business organization.
  • Business to Consumer (B2C): Businesses sell products or services directly to consumers through online platforms.
  • Consumer to Consumer (C2C): Consumers sell goods and services directly to other consumers using online marketplaces.
  • Consumer to Business (C2B): Individuals provide products or services to businesses electronically.
  • Business to Government (B2G): Electronic transactions take place between businesses and government organizations.

Advantages of E-Business Models

  • Global market accessibility
  • Reduction in operational costs
  • Fast communication and transactions
  • Better customer interaction
  • 24×7 business operations
  • Improved business efficiency

Conclusion

E-Business models have transformed modern business operations by enabling organizations to conduct activities electronically through digital platforms. Different models such as B2B, B2C, C2C, and C2B provide businesses with various opportunities for growth, communication, and revenue generation. As digital technologies continue to develop, E-Business models will become even more important in the global business environment.

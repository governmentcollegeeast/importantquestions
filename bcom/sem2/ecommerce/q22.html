Digital Signature
Government College Ludhiana East • E-Commerce — BBA Semester II Prepared by: Jeevansh Manocha

Introduction

Security is one of the most important requirements in E-Commerce and online business transactions. Businesses and customers exchange sensitive information through digital networks, which creates risks of fraud, hacking, and unauthorized access. To ensure authenticity and security in electronic transactions, digital signatures are widely used.

Meaning of Digital Signature

A Digital Signature is an electronic method used to verify the identity of the sender and ensure that electronic information has not been altered during transmission.

Definition: A Digital Signature is an electronic authentication technique that verifies the authenticity and integrity of digital messages or documents using cryptographic methods.

Features of Digital Signature

  • Provides authentication of users
  • Ensures data integrity
  • Supports secure electronic transactions
  • Prevents unauthorized modification of data
  • Provides non-repudiation in transactions

Working of Digital Signature

Electronic Document Created
↓
Digital Signature Generated Using Private Key
↓
Signed Document Sent Online
↓
Verification Using Public Key
↓
Authenticated & Secure Transaction

Importance of Digital Signature in E-Commerce

  • Provides security in online transactions
  • Authenticates identity of users
  • Protects confidential business information
  • Prevents online fraud and forgery
  • Improves customer trust in E-Commerce systems

Advantages of Digital Signature

  • Fast electronic authentication
  • Secure communication over networks
  • Reduction in paperwork
  • Legally accepted in many countries
  • Improves efficiency of business transactions

Limitations of Digital Signature

  • Requires technical infrastructure
  • Risk of private key theft
  • Need for proper cybersecurity systems
  • Technical complexity in implementation

Conclusion

Digital Signature is an important security tool used in E-Commerce and online communication systems. It helps verify the authenticity of users, ensures data integrity, and protects electronic transactions from fraud and unauthorized access. With the increasing growth of digital business activities, digital signatures have become essential for maintaining trust and security in the modern digital economy.

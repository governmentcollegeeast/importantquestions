About DegreeNotes

A simple platform focused on what actually matters in exams — nothing extra, nothing unnecessary.


What is DegreeNotes?

DegreeNotes is created to provide only important and relevant content for exams. Instead of going through full books or random notes, here you will find only what is actually needed.

The focus is simple — important questions, structured answers, and useful resources. No unnecessary topics, no time waste.

Why this platform was created

While preparing for exams, most students struggle to find the right content. Either there is too much irrelevant material or important topics are not clearly structured.

DegreeNotes was created to solve this problem — to give students a clear path of what to study and what to skip.

About the Creator

DegreeNotes is created by Jeevansh Manocha, a student at Government College Ludhiana (East), under Panjab University.

The idea behind this platform is simple — to help students focus only on important content and save time during exam preparation.

All questions and resources shared here are specifically for Panjab University students.

Note

This platform is built only for educational purposes. The goal is to simplify preparation and help students perform better in exams.
